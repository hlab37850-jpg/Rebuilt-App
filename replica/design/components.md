# Component specifications — provisional

- App shell: RTL toolbar, menu, drawer, selected navigation item; open/closed and long-label states.
- Buttons: primary, secondary, text and destructive; pressed/focused/disabled/loading; minimum 48dp touch target.
- Fields: text, numeric, currency, date, autocomplete and select; visible labels and inline validation.
- Data rows/tables: search, sort, empty/loading/error and long Arabic content.
- Invoice editor: party, date/number, currency/rate, line add/edit/remove, quantity, price, discount, tax and total.
- Dialogs: explicit confirmation for destructive financial actions; explain effects on stock and balances.
- Reports: date-range filters, sortable columns, currency-aware totals and export.
- Accessibility: RTL traversal, content descriptions, scalable text, visible focus and adequate contrast.

Measure dimensions and states from fresh reference screenshots before implementing visual parity. WCAG AA floor: 4.5:1 body text and 3:1 large text/focus boundaries.
