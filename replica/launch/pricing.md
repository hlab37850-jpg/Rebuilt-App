# Pricing research — not final

The Apple App Store listing surfaced an annual in-app purchase at USD 19.99 in one storefront. Prices are regional and can change; verify current Yemen/target-market pricing before setting a price.
Source: https://apps.apple.com/ye/app/id1638621302

No price is set. Validate local payment methods, cloud/support costs, tax and consumer requirements first.

Candidate tiers:
- Starter: single shop, core local records, invoices and basic reports.
- Business: advanced reports, multiple cash boxes/warehouses and exports.
- Team: users, permissions and sync only after those features exist.

Do not sell cloud sync, automatic backup or compliance features before they are implemented and tested. Billing and cancellation must be transparent.
