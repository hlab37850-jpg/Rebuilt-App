# Public feedback research

## Sample and limits
Seven visible review excerpts were recorded across Google Play, Apple App Store and one third-party Android listing. This is a small convenience sample, not the 100+ review study recommended by the skill. Do not infer prevalence. Google Play's listing snapshot showed roughly 3.38K reviews and 4.3 stars; this is listing metadata, not our sample count.

## Themes
1. Period closing and multi-user/distributor roles — one visible Google Play request; thin evidence.
2. Support responsiveness — one visible Google Play complaint; thin evidence.
3. Period-based profit reports — one App Store review; thin evidence.
4. Multiple cash boxes — one App Store question; thin evidence.
5. Cross-platform parity — one App Store review; thin evidence.
6. Account consistency after updates — one App Store review; thin evidence.
7. Inventory costing clarity — one third-party listing excerpt; lower confidence and requires workflow verification.

## Product implications
- Make period filters and profit reports first-class, with a documented costing policy.
- Use one ledger as the source of truth and test journal balancing.
- Link receipts/payments to the selected cash box.
- Add period lock only with safe reopen/reversal and audit controls.
- Treat support responsiveness as an operating process, not a UI claim.
- Multi-device sync is different from backup and needs conflict handling.
