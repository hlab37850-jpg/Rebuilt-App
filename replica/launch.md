## Pricing research
The Apple App Store listing surfaced an annual in-app purchase at USD 19.99 in one storefront. Prices are regional and may change; verify current Yemen/target-market pricing before setting a price.
Source: https://apps.apple.com/ye/app/id1638621302

No price is set. Validate local payment methods, service costs, tax and support costs first. Candidate tiers: Starter (single shop), Business (advanced reports and multiple locations), Team (users/permissions/sync only after implementation).

## Launch plan
- Finish must-have features and all S1/S2 fixes.
- Prepare privacy policy, terms, support contact and data-safety disclosures.
- Recruit opt-in beta testers; do not invent users or testimonials.
- Test on multiple Android versions and screen sizes.
- Capture real product screenshots; track crashes without logging financial personal data.
- Publish known limitations and release notes.
- No public launch until brand clearance, tests, listing/legal checks and release signing pass.
